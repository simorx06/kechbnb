You have just acquired an apartment in the medina or a riad in the palm grove, and the idea of listing it on Airbnb appeals to you. Tourist demand in Marrakech is real and sustained throughout the year. But between creating the listing, managing bookings and welcoming guests, there is a gap that many first-time landlords underestimate. This guide gives you the tools to make the leap methodically.

## 1. Perfect your listing before anything else

Your Airbnb listing is your shop window. In a market as competitive as Marrakech, a poorly written listing or one with mediocre photos is consistently overlooked. Here are the essential elements:

**Photos**: this is the number one factor in the booking decision. Hire a professional photographer or ensure you have generous natural light, wide angles and immaculately tidy spaces. Aim for at least 15 to 20 photos covering every room, the kitchen, the bathroom and any outdoor spaces.

**The title**: it should combine your property's main selling point and its location. "Authentic riad with private patio — heart of the medina" is infinitely more effective than "Nice riad Marrakech".

**The description**: write it with your target visitor in mind. Talk about the atmosphere, the proximity to the souks or top restaurants. Be precise about equipment, room sizes and access arrangements.

## 2. Set a price consistent with the market

Airbnb pricing is a discipline in its own right. In Marrakech, prices vary by season, local events (marathons, trade fairs, festivals) and property quality. As a general rule:

- **High season** (October to April): you can apply rates 30 to 50% higher than in low season.
- **Low season** (July–August): the market tightens — consider lowering your prices while maintaining your minimum profitability.
- **Local events**: the Marrakech Marathon, SIAM or major religious holidays justify one-off price spikes.

Activate Airbnb's dynamic pricing or use a third-party tool like Pricelabs to automate these adjustments.

## 3. Prepare a flawless welcome

The guest experience begins well before arrival. Respond to booking requests within the hour (Airbnb rewards responsive hosts in its algorithm). Send a clear welcome message with practical details: access code, wifi, useful numbers.

On arrival, a personal welcome — or via a trusted concierge — makes all the difference. In Marrakech, guests often come for an authentic cultural experience; take the time to recommend a few local addresses.

## 4. Manage reviews with care

Reviews are the engine of your visibility on Airbnb. A property with 20 reviews at 4.9 stars will always beat a brand-new listing with no track record. Systematically request reviews after every stay and respond to each one, positive or negative.

In the case of a negative review, keep your composure. Acknowledge the problem, explain the measures taken to address it and respond publicly and professionally. Future travellers read the responses as much as the reviews themselves.

## 5. Consider the regulations

Verify that your property complies with Moroccan rules for short-term tourist rentals. Declare your rental income and make sure you have home insurance covering tourist stays.
