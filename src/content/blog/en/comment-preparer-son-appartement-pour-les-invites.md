In the world of tourist accommodation, details create big differences. A guest who finds an impeccable, well-equipped property that "feels" like someone has cared for them will reliably leave a 5-star review. Conversely, one oversight — a missing bar of soap, a dubious bathroom mirror, patchy wifi — can ruin an otherwise successful experience. Here is how to prepare your property to a professional standard.

## 1. Cleanliness: the absolute fundamental

No amount of careful decoration compensates for a poorly cleaned property. Cleanliness is the number one satisfaction criterion for Airbnb travellers, well ahead of equipment or location.

**What your guests will inspect first:**
- The bathroom: grout, mirror, taps, toilet
- The kitchen: worktop, inside the fridge, oven or microwave
- Sheets and pillows: white preferably, spotless, neatly tucked
- Floors and room corners
- The general smell of the property

Invest in quality cleaning products and, where possible, use a professional team for the turnovers between stays. Every minute of rushed cleaning can cost tens of euros in negative reviews.

## 2. Equipment: what travellers expect

In Marrakech, your competitors often offer well-equipped properties. To stay competitive, you need to cover the essentials:

**Kitchen:** coffee maker (ideally pod-based), kettle, microwave, clean fridge, crockery sufficient for the stated guest capacity, salt, pepper, oil and a few basic condiments.

**Bathroom:** liquid soap (not a shared bar), shampoo, shower gel, clean bath towels in sufficient quantity, hair dryer.

**Living area:** smart TV or Chromecast, fast wifi (minimum 20 Mbps) with the code clearly displayed, USB chargers available or accessible multi-sockets.

**Air conditioning / fans:** in Marrakech, air conditioning is essential in summer. Make sure it works perfectly and that your guests know how to operate it.

## 3. Decoration: creating a memorable atmosphere

A thoughtfully designed property stands out in Airbnb search results and justifies a higher price. You do not need a massive budget — a few strategic choices are sufficient:

- **Textiles**: cushions in warm tones (ochre, terracotta, gold), a traditional Moroccan throw on the sofa, natural linen curtains.
- **Lighting**: favour ambient lamps over harsh ceiling lights. Zellige or copper lamps are accessible in Marrakech and add an authentic local touch.
- **Plants**: a green plant in the living room or on the terrace brings life and freshness.
- **Welcome tray**: a few dates, mint tea or local biscuits placed on the entrance table create an unforgettable first impression at no significant cost.

## 4. The welcome guide: essential

Prepare a welcome booklet (physical or digital) containing:
- The wifi code
- Instructions for equipment (air con, TV, coffee machine)
- Waste sorting instructions and collection days
- Your contact details or those of the property manager in case of emergency
- 5 to 10 personal recommendations: an authentic tajine restaurant, neighbourhood hammam, local bookshop…

This booklet reassures your guests from the moment they arrive and reduces the number of messages during their stay.

## 5. The small touches that generate great reviews

Airbnb travellers remember one thing: how they felt during their stay. A few simple gestures contribute to that feeling:

- A bottle of mineral water in the fridge on arrival
- A personalised handwritten welcome note
- A guide to local pharmacies and out-of-hours doctors
- The availability of local SIM cards or information on where to buy them

These attentions cost little and are worth a great deal in terms of loyalty and reviews.
